Changes by aotd with CAD assistance, November 2026:

- Measured CMP170HX end interface, aligned air windows, rounded entry edges and a tapered horizontal splitter; unnecessary vertical dividers removed.
- Smooth 75 mm transition, 5 mm collar and 4 mm fan plate; nominal 2 mm XY shell offset.
- Fan-side mounting lands enlarged while retaining the measured 28.5 mm GPU screw pitch.
- v5 moves the fan, collar and fan plate **22 mm physically upward** relative to the previously printed v4. The GPU attachment and axial length are unchanged. A new smooth transition joins them.
- Added print orientation, editable Bambu Studio project, checks, support previews and build scripts.

The A100 reference in the original title is not a compatibility claim for this measured CMP170HX remix. All third-party product manuals and marketing images are excluded from this distribution.
