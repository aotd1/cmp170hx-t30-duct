# Installation

1. With the PC powered down, trial-fit the GPU interface and confirm the two mounting holes, air openings and EPS cutout align. Do not use screws to force a misaligned print into position.
2. Remove supports and loose strands from the airway. Check the splitter, thin wall areas and mounting lands for damage.
3. Attach the T30 with the outlet side (rear label and motor support struts) facing the duct. Air travels from the fan through the adapter and heatsink to the opposite card end. Match the airflow arrow on the frame.
4. Use four suitable fan fasteners or rubber mounts; the fan plate is 4 mm thick. Do not force barbed rubber mounts through holes that cannot accommodate them. Check blade clearance.
5. Attach the adapter to the card using screws matched to its threads and engagement depth. The nominal GPU screw spacing is 28.5 mm. Tighten only enough to seat the adapter; a model-specific torque is not established.
6. Place an independent support under the fan frame/outer mounting region. Avoid loading the thin duct skin. Arrange EPS and other cables so they do not pull on the duct or enter the fan.
7. Check the full HDD insertion/removal path, upper clearances, case closure and all cables. The requested 22 mm fan lift is not a guarantee of clearance in another chassis.
8. Confirm airflow and monitor GPU temperature during a gradual functional test. This publication supplies no validated fan curve or thermal limit automation.

The 2 mm shell value is an XY offset, not a uniform thickness normal to every curved surface. Local wall thickness and printed paths vary. Do not assume higher-temperature service suitability from the PETG label alone.
